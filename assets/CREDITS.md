## Models

No third-party models. Every model in `assets/models/` is a placeholder built by `tools/build_placeholder_models.gd` from primitive shapes.

## Textures

No third-party textures. Terrain, sky bodies, stars and effects are generated by shaders and scripts in the project.

## Audio

No third-party audio. Every sound in `assets/audio/` is synthesised by `tools/build_placeholder_audio.py` from sine waves and noise.

## Fonts

The game uses the default font built into Godot (Open Sans, SIL Open Font Licence, shipped with the engine).
